## Evidence and scope

`release-report.json` is generated by `node qa/verify-release.mjs --collect`.
It checks exact gameplay/mutation preservation, old-version hashes, asset and
license integrity, source-loaded browser evidence and unchanged strict timing
thresholds. A report cannot pass merely because files exist or were stamped
with hashes at upload: the server binds loaded-source hashes at page request
and rejects stale uploads.

Actual evidence is exported from the WebGL canvas, not desktop screenshots.
The static images cover hero, courtyard, window interior, wet ground and SMG.
Three historical images sample actual combat before/during/after lightning.
Their loaded-source fingerprints predate the final sleeve fit, so they are
explicitly stale for final acceptance. Their wall-clock spacing can be extended
by background throttling; they are **not** normal-speed footage or proof of
continuous visual review. The verifier reports stale source bindings as failures.

No cover art is used as rendering evidence. The AI-generated social cover is
separate and explicitly labelled in its provenance and the in-game credits.

## Independent art direction

Planck screened actual hero/courtyard/interior images with the live-ops art
precheck. Initial verdict: REWORK, not UE5/AAA parity. Successful elements:
wet/dry ground breakup, weather atmosphere and warm light pools. Requested
changes: darker/coherent storm sky, legible room depth, smaller gun framing.

One bounded rework darkened sky gain, reduced glass specular/room-light exposure
and reduced MP5 display fit2.0->1.70. A separate procedural sleeve continuation
was tried and rejected during review. The final fit instead bakes the source
held arm pose and smoothly fits only the rear upper sleeve toward the offscreen
torso. Original continuous fabric, material and UV0/UV1 are retained; Float32
normals are recomputed. Source wrist/grip positions remain unchanged.

Remaining fidelity limits are material: repeated/simple facade treatment,
decorative parallax rooms, basic enemy movement/grip, no authored full reload,
and simplified held-pose arm animation. These are not eliminated by bloom, rain or
an engineering PASS. Final independent findings are in `art-review.json`.

The final representative hero-frame spot-check received **CONDITIONAL PASS for
WIP only**: the exposed sleeve join/cutoff is resolved. Remaining minor priorities
are rear-sleeve texture stretching, a stronger night read (currently stormy
twilight), facade repetition and uneven environmental detail. This bounded art
verdict does not accept source correctness, motion, performance, other poses,
or UE5/AAA parity; overall checkpoint acceptance remains REWORK.
