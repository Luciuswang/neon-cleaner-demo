**PASS, conditional.** The plan matches the brief, but "non-colliding" does not by itself protect sightlines or hit detection.

Hard conditions:
1. No new ledges, rubble, trees or tall grass on paths or in play lanes. Decoration there stays below player eye and hit height.
2. New meshes are excluded from gameplay raycasts and hit tests.
3. No rock that reads as cover without matching existing collision.
4. Placement is seeded and deterministic, with all geometry and textures generated in code.
5. A numeric frame-time and draw-call budget is fixed before work begins.
6. Any failure of collision or sightline parity reverts the change.
