Recorded firearm sources:
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Created and recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew
Nanney. The OpenGameArt submitter is `bart`. The preserved distribution labels
the archive CC0. Original team websites are no longer available.
Selected AR-15/M4 singles: `AR-15/D_32P.wav` (near) and `AR-15/D_24P.wav` (mid).
The archive's master sheet identifies firearm, distance and microphone position.

Mechanical reload sources:
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects

Author BMacZero; optional credit Brian MacIntosh. CC0.
`clipload1.wav`, `clipload2.wav`, `singlebullet1.wav`.
The author does not specify recording method. The last clip is used as a
quiet hit-confirmation UI cue, not described as a real bullet/body impact.

The original gunshots contain saturated source samples. Downmixing, trimming,
fades, resampling and reduced gain do not repair source saturation.
See `manifest.json` for exact archive/member/output hashes and transformations.
Preparation script: `../../tools/prepare-audio.py` relative to this directory.

Two recorded single-shot perspectives are used. Pitch/gain variation at runtime
does not constitute additional recorded takes. No gunshot oscillator fallback.
